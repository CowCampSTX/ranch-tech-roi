The one-liner

“Before you sign for collars, water monitors or EID gear, put in your own numbers and see whether it pays for itself, and what price would make it pay.”

To ranchers

  • Vendor-neutral: generic names, no sales agenda
  • Gives you the break-even price to take to the dealer
  • Your herd, your land, owned or leased, your labor
  • Free, in the browser, nothing uploaded

To lenders, agents, consultants

  • Every number traces to a formula and a source class
  • A shared link puts everyone on the same scenario
  • Operator’s share kept apart from the landlord’s
  • Before tax, stated plainly, never hidden
