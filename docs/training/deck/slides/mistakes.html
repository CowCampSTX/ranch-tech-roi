Catch these early

Four common mistakes

Counting freed hours as cash

Hours are cash only if someone you pay works less. Otherwise they are time back, shown apart.

Leaving the 7% alone

The page flags it until it is reviewed. Use loan rate plus risk, minus inflation.

Typing in a brochure’s savings

A vendor’s “typical savings” are not this ranch’s records. Use your own hours and losses.

Misreading the units

Collar subscriptions are often per head per month. Check the unit beside each price.

The first one is the big one. The tool keeps hours freed out of cash on purpose. If someone insists the time is worth money, the economic NPV values it at the owner time rate they enter.
