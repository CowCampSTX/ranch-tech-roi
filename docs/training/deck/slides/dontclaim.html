Keep it honest

What not to claim

Don’t saySay instead
“It does your taxes.”Every result is before tax. Ask your tax adviser about after-tax.
“That’s my ranch.”The examples are synthetic: made up for demonstration.
“It costs $X.”Vendor prices are dated examples. Get your own quote.
“Use 7%.”7% is an example rate. Review it for your operation.
“It will save you $X.”It shows what you would need to save for it to pay.

These match the rules the tool itself follows. Staying inside them keeps the pitch credible and keeps you clear of giving financial or tax advice.
