## 13. Sources

### 13.1 How these were checked

The sources were checked in three steps: research by topic, a separate claim verification, and a link check on
15 October 2026.

- **Research:** the sources were researched by topic: virtual fencing, water monitoring, EID and scales, shared
  economic inputs, and candidate modules.
- **Claim verification:** a separate, skeptical check reopened the sources behind 65 load-bearing claims and tried to
  refute each one. The counts below refer to that check. It was not repeated during the link check.
  - **Confirmed:** 61 of the 65 claims.
  - **Corrected:** 2 claims, which now carry the corrected wording in this document. One is the "often" in
    az2117's 2,080-hour finding. The other is the base of the NAHMS 3.5% figure.
  - **Unverifiable:** 2 claims: the outcome of the EID lawsuit after March 2026, and the conclusions of Hoag et al.
    2025 (abstract only).
- **Link and title check (15 October 2026):** every source cited in the text and in the evidence table has an ID
  below, except two unverified water-monitor listings in §4.2 (LoneStar; Tank Toad), which have no URL or date (see
  "Listings without a source" in 13.2). Each entry gives a URL or DOI, except three whose URL is not confirmed
  ([S37], [S70], [S72]). Each also gives a publication date, or the date a web page or retail listing was checked, or
  says that no date was found ("undated", "date unknown" or "not shown on the page"). URLs came from the notes kept during the research
  and the claim verification (not published), or were found by web search. For each one except [S109] and [S111]
  (search-result metadata only), the title was checked against a page opened during the research, the claim
  verification or the link check. The link check confirmed links, titles and dates. It did not re-verify the claims
  themselves.
  - **Corrected in the link check:** the titles of MLA B.GFB.1719 [S54] and Oklahoma State ANSI-3257 [S59]; the full
    title of NMSU B-132 [S7]; the author order of UF/IFAS FE1060 [S79], which now follows the page byline; and the
    credit on the Utah State water-monitoring page [S26], which names K. Heaton as a project participant, not as
    the author.
  - **Removed:** citations that had no identifiable source (a "Merck Vence page", a news report of cattle deaths in
    Australia, a 2021 quote about leaks, and a vendor claim of "up to 50%" fewer deaths) were taken out of the text
    and have no entry here.
- **Quotes:** quoted text is as extracted from each page or PDF. Re-check the exact wording against the original
  before quoting it in public material.
- **How the IDs are used:** the evidence table (5.3) cites sources by these IDs in its "Source IDs" column. Its CSV
  (`evidence-table.csv`) builds its URL column from this list, so the two always match.
