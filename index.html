Ranch Tech ROI

Will a piece of ranch technology pay for itself on your operation? Start from an example ranch, replace its numbers with your own quotes and records, and see the cash result, the payback, what would make it pay and which inputs the answer turns on. Every result is before tax, and every calculation is written out below the charts.

The four starting points are synthetic example ranches: made-up numbers for demonstration, not any real operation's data. Each example price is labeled as what it is: a published list price, a price reported by the press, or a synthetic placeholder with no published source (see Sources at the bottom). The benefits are assumptions to replace with your own records. Prices change, so get a quote before you decide.
